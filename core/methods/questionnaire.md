# A questionnaire for the owner or a named expert

For the decisions only the owner or a human expert can make.

1. **One file per recipient and occasion,** `factory/decisions/questions-<date>-<words>.md`: a title, its purpose, and one paragraph of context for someone who has not followed the work.
2. **How to answer:** the date it is needed by, the minutes it takes, and that "I don't know" is welcome.
3. **Questions under themed headings, most important first, one idea each.** Each holds the working answer the project is building on, the options with what each means in plain words, the recommendation and who makes it, and an answer line.
4. **Say what reverses or narrows something already decided,** beside the question that does it.
5. **Close** with one open question: what else should we know?

Check: every question names what it settles and the working answer in use; the whole file can be answered in the time it states.
